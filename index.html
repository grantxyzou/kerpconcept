Services

What we do.

We’re a barbershop and a salon in the same room. Men’s and women’s services both.

  • Cuts & fades

    Skin fades, tapers, scissor cuts, and tidying up something that’s grown out.

    from $55

  • Beard & shave

    Hot towel and a straight razor. Beard trims and shape-ups too.

    from $40

  • Colour

    Root touch-ups, highlights, grey blending, and fixing colour that didn’t go to plan. We’ll talk it through first.

    from $70

  • Perms & smoothing

    Cold perm, iron perm, and straightening. These take a while, so book ahead.

    from $130

Ricardo

Ricardo trained as a barber in Spain and has been at it for twenty-five years. Kerp was an idea he sat on for a long time before it became a room on West Pender.

He also teaches. Private classes and workshops for barbers who want to sharpen up their fades.
